Why this shape
  • Order by reachability. No "where are you?" question; two unauthenticated /models probes decide. VPN users get the honest answer too.
  • AvalAI is not assumed. It is a normal card with a plain label: Iranian provider, no VPN there, rial payment. Global users skip it.
  • No jargon before success. Base URL, model and voice live behind "Advanced". The only field is a key.
  • Free first. OpenRouter starts on a free model, and the picker can show free models only. No card is needed for the whole path: free translation, free English voice.
  • Voice splits by language. English pronunciation is the common need. The translation side falls back to the system voice when the provider has no voice for that language.
  • Errors name the next action. "Key rejected" ≠ "blocked on your network" ≠ "free daily limit reached".
Provider facts
  • OpenRouter: free :free models; 20 requests a minute and 50 a day, shared by translation and voice. A one-time $10 top-up raises the day to 1000. The API answers 403 from Iranian IPs.
  • AvalAI: OpenAI-compatible; 200k toman signup credit with phone verification; Gemini 3.8 TTS speaks Persian.
  • GitHub Models: 150 requests/day, text only; the system voice speaks.
  • Ollama + Piper: offline, free, English and Persian voice packs.
